Every kind of every weebo operator, plus External Secrets and cert-manager,
in one project: add resources, point each reference field at another
resource (or at the Secret one of them makes), and export the whole set as
YAML. Start from a recipe to see how the operators fit together. The list
of kinds, and the versions they come from, is
[`site/crd-catalog.config.mjs`](https://github.com/batleforc/weebo-operator-kit/blob/main/site/crd-catalog.config.mjs).

An operator's references show once it is released on a kit with
`links.json` (v0.23 on); until then its kinds are listed without them.

Some kinds are **schematic**: an operator declares them for the drawing
alone (a cluster and the ways to reach it, the upstreams a registry
mirrors). They are edited and linked like the others, some appear on their
own from a resource's fields, and they are never applied: they are left
out of the manifests and downloaded apart as `schematic.yaml`.
